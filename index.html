SGTransit privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

SGTransit shows Singapore bus arrival times, routes and train information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. This page says what the app does with your information.

What stays on your phone

  • Your saved stops, the buses you pin, your Home and Work places, recent searches and your settings.
  • Your location, while the app uses it to show nearby stops and the map. It is not stored by the app.

None of this is backed up or sent to the developer.

What the app sends, and why

To show live information, the app asks these services for it:

  • Bus and train data (Land Transport Authority, through the developer's server): the codes of the bus stops and train lines you are looking at. No location is sent for this.
  • Trip planning and place search (OneMap, Singapore Land Authority, through the developer's server): the text you search for, and the start and end points of a trip you plan. If you plan from "Your location", the start point is your current location.
  • Maps (OneMap): the map pictures for the area on screen.
  • Google Maps: only if you tap "View in Google Maps"; the trip's start and destination are passed to the Google Maps app.

The developer's server only passes requests on to these services. It does not keep your requests, other than the short-lived technical logs that its hosting provider (Cloudflare) keeps to run the service.

Permissions

  • Location: to show stops near you, point the map, and plan trips from where you are. You can say no; the app then shows central Singapore.
  • Notifications: for bus arrival alerts and leave reminders that you set.

Children

SGTransit is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/blankshiro/sgtransit-privacy/issues.
